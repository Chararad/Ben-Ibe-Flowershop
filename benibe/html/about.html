Rooted in 1985. Growing through generations.

Our story

began in 1985, when Bienvenido “Ben” Ibe and his wife, Purita “Rita” Ibe, started a humble flower business in front of the Holy Rosary Parish Church (HRPC) in Sto. Rosario, Angeles City. With a simple cartella (gariton) and pails of flowers, they began with little more than determination, hard work, and a deep passion for flowers.

From a “gariton,” they eventually acquired a permanent stall, still in front of HRPC. Through the years, the shop was known by different names, including Ben & Rita Flower Shop and Ben Rita Flower Shop, before eventually becoming Ben Ibe Flowers.

The journey was not always smooth. There were challenges, difficult seasons, and moments that tested their perseverance. Yet, by the grace of God, they were able to overcome each season and move forward. What began as a small flower cart gradually became a lasting legacy built on dedication, sacrifice, commitment, and the values of its founders.

Today, the original store remains in front of HRPC, while our main store at Bale Herencia in San Jose, Angeles City, opened in 2022. It has since been expanded to serve as a showroom, administrative space, and production area, supporting the growing demand for our online flower services.

Although Nanay Rita has passed on, the values she and Tatay Ben instilled remain at the heart of what we do. At 90 years old, Tatay Ben continues to be an important part of our story.

Their children now help carry this heritage forward. Alongside Ben Ibe Flowers are our sister stores, managed by their sons and daughters. Each has its own identity while remaining connected to the roots from which they all began.

More Than Flowers

At the heart of Ben Ibe Flowers is a purpose that goes beyond what we create and sell. We believe in helping, sharing, and giving—serving our customers, providing opportunities for those who work with us, supporting families, and extending a helping hand to others whenever we can.

Generosity is a value that Tatay Ben and Nanay Rita passed down through the years. It reminds us that success is not measured only by how much we grow, but also by how many lives we are able to touch along the way.

As we look ahead, we hope to remain true to that purpose—to make every moment special with flowers while making a meaningful difference in the lives of the people around us.

From a simple cartella in 1985 to a growing family of flower shops today, we are grateful for how far God has brought us.

By God’s grace, the journey and legacy continues.
