Our Services

Beyond our everyday arrangements and gifts, Ben Ibe Flowers brings the joy of flowers to your celebrations and gatherings. Add a Bloom Cart to your event for a hands-on flower experience, or join a Flower Workshop to learn how to arrange with our team. Send us your booking and we'll take care of the rest!

Bloom Carts

Bloom Carts bring the flower shop to your event, with a beautifully styled cart of fresh stems, wrapping, and ribbons for guests to build their own bouquets. Our florists are on hand to help everyone pick their blooms, so each guest leaves with something they love. It's a warm, memorable touch for weddings, birthdays, showers, graduations, and company events, and it can be matched to your theme and color palette.

What’s included

  • A styled Bloom Cart set up at your venue, with pickup after the event
  • Fresh stems, wrapping, and ribbons for guests to build their own bouquets
  • A Ben Ibe Flowers florist on hand to help guests choose and arrange

Flower Workshops

Flower Workshops are hands-on classes where you learn to arrange fresh flowers with guidance from the Ben Ibe Flowers team, with no experience needed. You'll practice choosing stems, balancing color and shape, and wrapping your finished piece, then take your creation home. Join with friends and family, or book a private group session for birthdays, showers, team-building days, and other celebrations.

What’s included

  • Fresh flowers and all the materials you need, including wrapping and ribbons
  • Step-by-step guidance from our florists, with no experience needed
  • Your finished arrangement to take home
